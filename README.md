## 📈 Commercial Gateway: LEAK-AUDIT Express Channel Verification

**Operational Protocol Standard:** `LEAK-AUDIT-v1.0.0`  
**Application Layer:** Risk Mitigation & Distribution Channel Integrity Audit  

The Founding Principal offers senior corporate clients and cross-border trade federations a rapid, non-invasive financial verification matrix to identify hidden margin leakage within complex distribution supply chains.

### 🔍 1. Data Requirements (Zero-Friction Onboarding)
To execute the cryptographic verification loop, the client provides only **three absolute metrics** in a single currency for the designated operational period:
*   **R** — Total Channel Revenue
*   **D** — Direct Channel Costs
*   **F** — Full Cumulative Costs (including logistics, compliance, local duties, and FX friction)

*Note: No deep ledger access or internal account disclosure required. Only three dynamic inputs.*

### 📊 2. Algorithmic Outputs & Verifiable Deliverables
Upon compilation, the platform generates a deterministic validation report containing:
1.  **System Verdict:** `GREEN` (Proceed) / `YELLOW` (Audit Trigger) / `RED` (Fail-Secure Emergency Stop).
2.  **Absolute Leakage Matrix:** Quantified financial loss in baseline currency.
3.  **Relative Volatility Index:** Percentage-based deviation relative to total revenue.
4.  **Cryptographic Invariant Seal:** A immutable hex signature securing the structural integrity of the audit state.

### 🛡️ 3. Execution Thresholds
*   🟩 **GREEN** (Leakage < 1%): Operational clearance. Hash committed to the verification chain.
*   🟨 **YELLOW** (1% ≤ Leakage < 5%): Automated document verification request (5-day SLA).
*   🟥 **RED** (Leakage ≥ 5%): Immediate process isolation, risk escalation, and operational shutdown (`FAIL_SECURE`).
